This is the Explain page. Pick a question, or type your own, and press Explain my week. Watch each step appear live: the Planner chooses the queries, code runs them against Zetaris or the local ledger, the Analyst writes findings where every number cites the query behind it, the Evaluator checks each claim against the rows and scores the draft out of nine, and the Writer produces the final review. The Proof cards count the sponsor work: queries run through Zetaris, memories recalled by the H-MEM engine, Nemotron model calls with their token counts, and the guards that checked or blocked. Click a citation tag to open the exact rows behind a number, and the SQL that Zetaris ran. At the bottom, the Correct this box lets you teach the next review what to do differently.
